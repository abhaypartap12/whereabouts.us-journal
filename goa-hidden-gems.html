Goa's Best Kept Secrets

Discover the Goa that locals hide—uncrowded, unspoiled, and captured in our own frames.

Crucial Rule Before You Visit

These spots are pristine because they are less discovered. Please respect the local vibe. DO NOT litter. Collect all your garbage (bottles, wrappers, everything) and bring it back with you. Leave no trace!

Peaceful Cliff View

If you’re in South Goa and looking for a quiet, stunning cliff view, this is the absolute best one. You can drive almost all the way to the edge of the cliff. Very little crowd, pure peace.

The Stairway Beach

If you can manage a small trek of about ~100 stairs, this beach is an absolute must-visit. The effort is highly rewarding with gorgeous views and a secluded shore.

Tip: Don’t go to Butterfly Beach—it's a total waste of time and effort compared to this spot!
